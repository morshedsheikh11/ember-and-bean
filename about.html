About us

Our story

A small neighbourhood café that roasts its own beans and cooks everything fresh.

How it began

A small café with a very warm roaster.

Ember & Bean began with a second-hand 5 kg drum roaster and a shoplot in Bangsar. Every morning before the doors open, we roast the day's beans in the back room, and the smell finds its way to the street.

Our kitchen works the same way. Bread is baked here, sambal is cooked down slowly, and desserts are made in small batches until they run out. Stay for a long lunch, bring your laptop, bring your mother. There's always a table worth keeping.

  • Locally sourcedFarms and bakers we know

How we brew

Four steps, no shortcuts.

  1. Sourcing

    We buy direct from small farms, from Sabah to Sidamo.

  2. Roasting

    Small batches every morning, profiled for each origin.

  3. Brewing

    Weighed, timed and tasted by our baristas all day.

  4. Serving

    Poured fresh and brought to your table, still warm.
